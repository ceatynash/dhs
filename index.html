AI Student Register Import (PDF)

Upload a searchable PDF register for up to 800 students. AI extracts the fields, checks DHS IDs against existing records, and prepares a review list. Existing records are never overwritten.

Developer only
Privacy notice: readable PDF text containing student details is sent to the AI provider configured on Render for extraction. After you confirm an import, each student's initial PIN and basic portal access details are sent individually by email only (SMTP) to the extracted parent email. Verify every email address against school records first. Notifications remain disabled until PARENT_NOTIFICATIONS_ENABLED=true and SMTP email is configured in Render. Records without a valid email address, or when email delivery fails, are not sent by another channel. The PDF itself is processed in memory and is not saved by this feature. Scanned/image-only PDFs require OCR first.

No import preview loaded. The AI API key and model must be configured on Render.
